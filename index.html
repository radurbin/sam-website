About Me Headshot & Résumé Media Gallery Contact Me

What's up y'all, welcome to my website. My name is Sam Yousuf, born and raised down South in the Peach state, I am a Georgia boy through and through. I just wrapped up my BFA in Musical Theater at the University of Cincinnati College - Conservatory of Music (CCM). I am a proud first generation Ethiopian - American with a HUGE family that means the world to me. My first love was singing R&B and soul, but it wasn't until I got to high school that I found a lasting love for acting and storytelling. Somehow it was my suburban school's production of Hairspray that opened my eyes to the magic of theater.

In addition to musical theater, I love film and TV (follow my letterboxd @samyousuf), straight plays, eating, playing every sport under the sun, and making music. At my core I am an activist and a believer in art being the way that I am going to change the world. I love my mom, my sisters, my friends, and I can't wait to get life going in New York City.

Thanks so much for checking out my website, lots of love!
